Questions before you enquire?

How do I request an appointment?

Complete the enquiry form above. WhatsApp will open with your details and selected service so you can send your request.

Can I ask about a specific service?

Yes. Select Spiritual Healing, Vedic Astrology, Yantra, Mantra & Tantra, Shakti Kriya or general guidance in the enquiry form.

Does submitting the form confirm an appointment?

No. It sends an enquiry through WhatsApp. The appointment is not confirmed until you receive a response from the center.
